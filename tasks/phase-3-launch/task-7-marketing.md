## Overview

This task focuses on developing and executing a comprehensive marketing and promotion strategy to launch the NEPHELE hotel management system to the market. The goal is to build awareness, generate leads, and establish market presence while driving customer acquisition.

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Low conversion rates | High | A/B testing, continuous optimization |
| Budget constraints | High | Prioritize high-ROI channels, organic content |
| Slow lead generation | Medium | Multiple channel approach, referral program |
| Negative reviews | High | Proactive reputation management, support |
| Market saturation | Medium | Differentiate on unique features, ROI focus |
