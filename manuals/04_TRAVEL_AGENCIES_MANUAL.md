## Getting Started

### About Nephele For Travel Agencies

Nephele provides travel agencies with:
- **Easy Booking System**: Create and manage reservations quickly
- **Client Management**: Store and access client information
- **Special Rates**: Access to travel agency rates and discounts
- **Commission Tracking**: Track earnings and commissions
- **Reporting**: Detailed reports on bookings and revenue
- **Multi-Property Access**: Book across multiple hotel properties

### What You Need

- **Travel Agency Account**: Set up by your agency account manager
- **Internet Access**: Stable connection for bookings
- **Client Information**: Guest details for making reservations
- **Payment Method**: Agency billing or credit card on file

## Logging In

### Initial Login

You'll receive from your agency account manager:
- **Agency Username**: Usually agency name or code
- **Agency Password**: Temporary password for first login
- **Access URL**: Nephele system login page

**First Login Process**:

1. Go to Nephele login page
2. Enter **Travel Agency Username**
3. Enter **Temporary Password**
4. Click **"Sign In"**

**Screenshot Reference**: See `sign_in_up.jpg` in Screenshots folder

### Setting Up Your Profile

After initial login, you'll set up:
1. **Business Email**: Your agency's email address
2. **Contact Person Name**: Your name or primary contact
3. **Phone Number**: Your agency's phone number
4. **Default Commission Structure**: How you prefer to handle commissions
5. **Billing Information**: How your agency will pay

### Changing Your Password

After first login, create a secure password:

1. Click your profile name (top-right corner)
2. Select **"Account Settings"**
3. Click **"Change Password"**
4. Enter current temporary password
5. Enter new password (strong password recommended)
6. Confirm new password
7. Click **"Update Password"**

### Multiple Users (Team Access)

**If your agency has multiple agents**:

1. Primary account holder can create additional user accounts
2. Go to **"Agency Management"** → **"Team Members"**
3. Click **"Add Team Member"**
4. Enter team member's email and details
5. Select permission level (Booking agent, Manager, Admin)
6. Click **"Invite"**
7. Team member receives email with login instructions

## Creating Bookings

### Booking Process Overview

**Step-by-step booking creation**:
1. Select property
2. Enter guest information
3. Choose room type
4. Select dates
5. Calculate pricing
6. Select payment method
7. Confirm booking
8. Send confirmation to guest

### Starting a New Booking

**Create a Booking**:

1. Click **"New Booking"** or **"Create Booking"** button
2. System opens booking form
3. Or: Go to **"Bookings"** → **"Create New Booking"**

**Step 1: Select Property**

1. Choose from dropdown or available properties list
2. If you don't know property name, you can search by:
   - Location/City
   - Country
   - Amenities
   - Price range
3. See property details including:
   - Available rooms
   - Current rates
   - Property information
   - Guest reviews/ratings

**Step 2: Enter Guest Information**

For new guests:

1. Click **"New Guest"** or **"Add Guest"**
2. Fill in guest details:
   - **First Name**: Guest's first name
   - **Last Name**: Guest's last name
   - **Email Address**: Valid email (important for confirmations)
   - **Phone Number**: Guest's phone number
   - **Country**: Country of residence
   - **Address**: Guest's mailing address
   - **ID Type & Number**: Passport or ID information
   - **Nationality**: Guest's nationality

3. Submit and continue

**For Returning Guests**:

1. Click **"Existing Guest"** or **"Select Guest"**
2. Search by:
   - Name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Booking reference

3. Click guest to select
4. System auto-fills their information
5. Continue to next step

**Step 3: Select Dates & Room**

1. Enter **Check-in Date**: When guest will arrive
2. Enter **Check-out Date**: When guest will depart
3. System shows available room types with:
   - Room type name (Single, Double, Suite, etc.)
   - Room capacity
   - Price per night
   - Total for stay duration
   - Room amenities

4. Click on room type to select
5. Add options if available:
   - Extra beds
   - Crib/baby equipment
   - Accessibility features
   - Room location preference (high floor, quiet side, etc.)

**Step 4: Review Pricing**

System shows:

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| **Room Rate** | Price per night |
| **Number of Nights** | Duration of stay |
| **Subtotal** | Room charges before taxes |
| **Taxes/Fees** | Government taxes and fees |
| **Travel Agency Discount** | Your agency's rate benefit |
| **Total Price** | Final amount guest pays |
| **Commission** | Your agency's earnings on this booking |

**Step 5: Add Special Requests**

1. In **"Special Requests"** section, add:
   - Room preference (high floor, quiet, near elevator, etc.)
   - Accessibility needs
   - Special occasions (honeymoon, anniversary, birthday)
   - Dietary requirements
   - Early check-in/late checkout request
   - Additional services needed

2. These requests are sent to the hotel

**Step 6: Select Payment Method**

Choose how guest will pay:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Credit Card** | Guest provides credit card details |
| **Agency Bill** | Bill to your agency account |
| **Bank Transfer** | Guest transfers funds |
| **Check** | Guest pays by check (if arranged) |

If **Credit Card**:
1. Collect guest's credit card information
2. System securely processes payment
3. Guest receives verification email
4. Booking confirmed immediately

If **Deferred Payment**:
1. Not charged until specific date or check-in
2. Guest receives payment reminder

**Step 7: Confirm Booking**

1. Review all booking details one final time:
   - Guest name and contact
   - Dates and room type
   - Total price and commission
   - Special requests
   - Payment method

2. Click **"Confirm Booking"** or **"Create Booking"**
3. System shows confirmation number
4. Booking confirmation email sent to guest
5. Copy confirmation number to your records

### Booking Confirmation Details

After booking created, you get:

- **Booking Reference Number**: Unique ID for tracking
- **Confirmation Email**: Sent to guest and you
- **Guest Itinerary**: Details of reservation
- **Payment Receipt**: If payment processed
- **Hotel Contact Information**: Property contact details

**Screenshot Reference**: See email confirmation examples in verification/confirmation files

## Managing Client Information

### Client Database

Your **Client Management** section allows you to:
- Store client information
- Track client preferences
- Access booking history
- Create client groups

### Adding Clients

**Add New Client to Your System**:

1. Go to **"Clients"** from menu
2. Click **"Add New Client"** or **"Import Client"**
3. Fill in client details:
   - **Name**: First and last name
   - **Email**: Email address
   - **Phone**: Contact number
   - **Address**: Mailing address
   - **Nationality**: Country of citizenship
   - **ID Information**: Passport/ID number type

4. Optional client information:
   - **Client Type**: Leisure, Business, Group, etc.
   - **Preferences**: Room type, amenities, location preferences
   - **Special Needs**: Accessibility requirements
   - **Loyalty Program**: Enrollment if applicable

5. Click **"Create Client"** or **"Save"**

### Client Preferences

Help guests get better experiences:

1. Open client profile
2. Click **"Edit Preferences"**
3. Note their preferences:
   - **Room Type Preference**: Type they like
   - **Floor Preference**: Ground floor, high floor, etc.
   - **Amenities**: What's important to them
   - **Service Level**: Standard, superior, luxury
   - **Dietary Restrictions**: Food allergies or preferences
   - **Special Needs**: Accessibility, medical equipment, etc.

4. Save preferences
5. When you book for them, system suggests best options

### Client Booking History

**View Client's Past Bookings**:

1. Open client profile
2. Click **"Booking History"** or **"Previous Bookings"**
3. See:
   - Past property stays
   - Dates of previous bookings
   - Rooms used
   - Amounts paid
   - Satisfaction feedback (if available)

4. Use history to:
   - Suggest similar properties
   - Remember their preferences
   - Identify loyalty for specials

### Bulk Client Import

**If you have many clients (large file)**:

1. Go to **"Clients"** → **"Import Clients"**
2. Prepare Excel/CSV file with these columns:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email
   - Phone
   - Address
   - Nationality
   - ID Type
   - ID Number

3. Upload file
4. System verifies data
5. Click **"Confirm Import"**

## Booking Management

### Viewing Your Bookings

**See All Your Bookings**:

1. Go to **"Bookings"** from menu
2. Default shows active/upcoming bookings
3. See table with:
   - Guest name
   - Check-in and check-out dates
   - Property name
   - Room type
   - Total cost
   - Booking status
   - Confirmation number

### Booking Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Awaiting payment or confirmation |
| **Confirmed** | Paid and locked in |
| **Cancelled** | Booking cancelled (by guest or you) |
| **Completed** | Stay is finished |
| **No-Show** | Guest didn't arrive |

### Filtering & Searching

**Find specific bookings**:

Use filters:
- **Date Range**: Bookings for specific dates
- **Status**: View only confirmed, pending, cancelled, etc.
- **Guest Name**: Search by name or partial name
- **Booking Reference**: Use confirmation number
- **Property**: Filter by hotel
- **Your Team Member**: See bookings by specific agent

**Example**: Search for "Smith" and status "Confirmed" to see all John Smith confirmed bookings

### Modifying Bookings

**Change Room Type** (if available):

1. Open booking
2. Click **"Modify Booking"**
3. Select **"Change Room"**
4. Choose different room from available options
5. Price adjusts automatically
6. Process any additional payment if new room more expensive
7. Click **"Confirm Change"**

**Extend Stay**:

1. Open booking
2. Click **"Extend Stay"** or **"Modify Booking"**
3. Select new check-out date
4. System calculates additional charges
5. Process payment for additional nights
6. Click **"Confirm Extension"**

**Shorten Stay**:

1. Open booking
2. Click **"Modify Booking"**
3. Change check-out date to earlier date
4. If refund owed (paid in advance), calculate refund amount
5. Process refund if applicable
6. Click **"Confirm"**

**Add Special Requests**:

1. Open active booking
2. Click **"Add Special Request"** or **"Edit Special Requests"**
3. Add additional guest requests
4. Note if request requires additional cost
5. Click **"Save"**

### Cancelling Bookings

**Cancel a Booking**:

1. Open booking
2. Click **"Cancel Booking"**
3. System shows refund amount based on:
   - Cancellation policy
   - How far in advance
   - When payment was made
4. Confirm cancellation
5. Refund processed if owed
6. Guest receives cancellation email
7. Booking status changes to "Cancelled"

**Refund Details**:
- Full refund if within free cancellation period
- Partial refund if past certain dates
- No refund if very close to arrival date
- Non-refundable bookings typically give 0 refund

### Resending Booking Confirmation

**Send Confirmation to Guest Again**:

1. Open booking
2. Click **"Resend Confirmation"** or **"Email Confirmation"**
3. System sends booking details to guest email
4. Guest receives all information about stay

## Commission & Payment Tracking

### Understanding Your Commissions

**Commission Basics**:

- Commission = Your agency's earnings on the booking
- Usually calculated as percentage of booking value
- Different commission rates for different properties/rates
- Paid to your agency, not individual agents

### Viewing Commission Details

**See Commission on Each Booking**:

1. Open any booking
2. In pricing section, see:
   - **Booking Total**: What guest pays
   - **Commission**: Your agency's earnings
   - **Commission %**: Percentage rate applied
   - **Amount Earned**: Dollar amount of commission

**Example**: If booking is $1,000 and commission is 10%, you earn $100

### Commission Reports

**View All Commissions Earned**:

1. Go to **"Commissions"** or **"Commission Reports"**
2. See all commissions from your bookings
3. Filter by:
   - Date range
   - Property
   - Team member (if applicable)
   - Status (Earned, Paid, Pending)

4. See totals:
   - **Your Period Earnings**: Commission earned this month/period
   - **Pending Payment**: Commission not yet paid
   - **Already Paid**: Commission you already received
   - **Total Lifetime**: Total all-time earnings

### Commission Schedule

**When Commissions Are Paid**:

1. Commission is "Earned" when booking is created
2. Commission is marked "Pending" until conditions met (guest checks in, etc.)
3. Commission is "Paid" when transferred to your agency account

**Typical Payment Schedule**:
- Monthly: Commissions paid monthly (around 1st of month)
- Upon Check-out: After guest checks out
- Quarterly: Paid every 3 months
- Custom: Per your agreement

Check your **"Payment Terms"** in agency settings for details

### Commission Breakdown by Property

**See Commission by Hotel**:

1. Go to **"Commissions"** → **"By Property"**
2. See:
   - Which properties earning most commission
   - Which properties have best rates
   - Trends in booking patterns
   - Which properties to prioritize

3. Use this data to guide your selling strategy

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Q: I created a booking but guest never received confirmation email.
**A**:
1. Check guest email address is correct
2. Ask guest to check spam/promotions folder
3. Go to booking and click **"Resend Confirmation"**
4. If still not received, contact Nephele support

### Q: A guest wants to book but I see "No Availability" for their dates.
**A**:
1. Those dates may be fully booked at that property
2. Suggest alternative dates nearby
3. Suggest alternative property in same location
4. Contact hotel directly if you think error

### Q: When will I receive my commission payment?
**A**:
1. Check your **"Settings"** → **"Commission Structure"** for payment schedule
2. Go to **"Commissions"** to see amounts and status
3. Status shows "Pending", "Approved", or "Paid"
4. Contact your agency manager about payment timing

### Q: A client has special accessibility needs. How do I note this?
**A**:
1. Open client profile
2. Click **"Edit Preferences"**
3. Add specific accessibility requirements in notes
4. Write detailed description of needs
5. When booking, add these to **"Special Requests"**
6. Hotel staff will accommodate

### Q: Can I see commission rates for different properties?
**A**:
1. Yes, go to **"Settings"** → **"Commission Structure"** or **"Rates"**
2. See all properties and their commission percentages
3. Use this to recommend higher-earning bookings
4. Rates may vary by season and booking type

### Q: I need to change a guest's check-in date. Can I do that?
**A**:
1. Open booking
2. Click **"Modify Booking"**
3. Change check-in and/or check-out dates
4. System shows new pricing
5. Process any additional payment needed
6. Click **"Confirm"**
7. Guest receives updated confirmation

### Q: How long does it take for a booking to be "Confirmed"?
**A**:
1. If guest pays by credit card: Immediately
2. If deferred payment: After payment is received
3. If agency pays: When you process payment to Nephele
4. Status updates in real-time in system

### Q: Can I book for multiple guests in one room?
**A**:
1. Yes, room capacity shows number allowed
2. Create booking under primary guest name
3. In special requests, note other guests
4. Or add additional guests in system if option available

### Q: What happens if a guest cancels?
**A**:
1. Go to booking and click **"Cancel Booking"**
2. System calculates refund based on cancellation policy
3. Refund is processed to guest
4. Your commission is reversed/deducted
5. Booking status changes to "Cancelled"

### Q: I accidentally entered wrong guest email. Can I fix it?
**A**:
1. Open booking
2. Click **"Edit"** or **"Modify Booking"**
3. Update guest email address
4. System will send corrected confirmation
5. Or manually contact guest with correct information

### Q: Can I add notes to a booking?
**A**:
1. Open booking
2. Look for **"Internal Notes"** or **"Booking Notes"**
3. Add your own notes for reference
4. These are private and not sent to guest
5. Use to remember important details

### Q: How do I give feedback about a property?
**A**:
1. Go to property listing or details
2. Click **"Rate Property"** or **"Leave Feedback"**
3. Enter your experience with that property
4. Rating helps other agents
5. Submit feedback

### Q: What should I do if the system seems broken or has errors?
**A**:
1. Refresh the web page
2. Try again or try different browser
3. Clear your browser cache and cookies
4. Restart your computer if problems persist
5. Contact Nephele technical support with details
