# Nephele Hotel Management System - User Manuals

Welcome to the comprehensive Nephele Hotel Management System user manuals repository. This directory contains detailed guides for each user role in the system.

## 📚 Available Manuals

### 1. [Admin Manual](01_ADMIN_MANUAL.md)
**For**: System Administrators

Complete guide to system-wide administration including:
- User and employee account management
- Property configuration and setup
- System settings and email configuration
- Reporting and analytics
- User role management
- System maintenance and oversight

**Key Topics**:
- Creating and managing accounts
- Setting up properties and rooms
- Configuring system settings
- Generating reports
- Managing user permissions

**Best for**: System administrators, IT staff, and anyone managing multiple properties or system-wide functions

---

### 2. [Property Manager Manual](02_PROPERTY_MANAGERS_MANUAL.md)
**For**: Hotel Managers and Property Supervisors

Comprehensive guide to managing individual hotel properties including:
- Staff scheduling and management
- Guest check-in and check-out processes
- Room inventory and status management
- Booking and reservation handling
- Event and announcement management
- Property-specific reporting and analytics

**Key Topics**:
- Managing daily operations
- Coordinating staff
- Handling guest issues
- Managing room inventory
- Creating and tracking events
- Analyzing property performance

**Best for**: Hotel managers, property managers, and operations supervisors

---

### 3. [Staff Members Manual](03_STAFF_MEMBERS_MANUAL.md)
**For**: Hotel Employees and Operational Staff

User-friendly guide for housekeeping, maintenance, food service, and general staff including:
- Account creation and login
- Viewing work schedules
- Managing assigned tasks
- Time tracking and attendance
- Submitting leave requests
- Reading announcements and news
- Performance tracking

**Key Topics**:
- Logging in and account setup
- Understanding your schedule
- Completing work tasks
- Requesting time off
- Communicating with management
- Performance reviews

**Best for**: Housekeeping staff, maintenance personnel, kitchen staff, receptionists, and all hotel employees

---

### 4. [Travel Agencies Manual](04_TRAVEL_AGENCIES_MANUAL.md)
**For**: Travel Agent Partners

Professional guide for travel agency partners booking through Nephele including:
- Agency account setup and team management
- Creating and managing bookings
- Client information management
- Commission tracking and reporting
- Agency-specific analytics
- Multi-property booking capabilities

**Key Topics**:
- Creating reservations for clients
- Managing client database
- Tracking commissions earned
- Generating agency reports
- Managing team members
- Accessing special rates and deals

**Best for**: Travel agents, travel agencies, corporate travel coordinators, and travel partners

---

### 5. [Guests Manual](05_GUESTS_MANUAL.md)
**For**: Hotel Guests

Guest-friendly guide to using Nephele during their stay including:
- Creating and managing guest account
- Viewing and modifying reservations
- Requesting room services
- Browsing hotel events and activities
- Managing bills and payments
- Communicating with hotel staff

**Key Topics**:
- Account creation and login
- Viewing booking details
- Requesting services
- Registering for events
- Understanding charges
- Checking out

**Best for**: Hotel guests and anyone making a reservation through the system

## 🔑 Key Features Across All Roles

### Universal Features

All users in Nephele can:
- ✅ Create and secure their account
- ✅ Log in from any compatible device/browser
- ✅ Update their profile information
- ✅ Change their password
- ✅ Recover forgotten passwords
- ✅ Access help and FAQs
- ✅ Submit feedback and reports

### Role-Specific Features

**Admins** can additionally:
- Create and manage all user accounts
- Configure system-wide settings
- Access comprehensive analytics
- Manage properties and rooms
- Create system announcements

**Property Managers** can additionally:
- Manage staff at their property
- Handle guest check-ins/outs
- Create events and announcements
- Generate property reports
- Configure property-specific settings

**Staff** can additionally:
- View their schedule
- Access assigned tasks
- Request time off
- Clock in/out
- Provide performance feedback

**Travel Agents** can additionally:
- Create and manage bookings
- Manage client database
- Track commissions
- Generate agency reports
- Access special rates

**Guests** can additionally:
- View booking details
- Request room services
- Register for events
- View and pay bills
- Modify reservations
