## Getting Started

### What is Nephele?

Nephele is our hotel's online guest portal designed to make your experience better. Use it to:
- Check your booking details anytime
- Request services or make changes to your stay
- Browse upcoming events and activities
- View your bills and invoices
- Get announcements about hotel news and offers
- Communicate with our staff about your needs

### System Benefits

- **24/7 Access**: Use anytime from any device
- **Quick Service Requests**: Get help without calling
- **Real-Time Updates**: See your bill and bookings instantly
- **Easy Communication**: Message hotel staff directly
- **Personal Preferences**: Save your preferences for future visits

### What You'll Need

- **Email Address**: To create account
- **Valid Password**: Secure your account
- **Internet Access**: Computer, tablet, or smartphone
- **Booking Information**: Confirmation number from reservation

## Creating Your Account

### Sign Up for Nephele

**New Guest Registration**:

1. Visit the Nephele login page (provided by hotel)
2. Click **"Sign Up"** or **"Create Account"**
3. Choose **"Guest"** as account type

**Screenshot Reference**: See `sign_in_up.jpg` in Screenshots folder

4. Fill in your information:
   - **First Name**: Your first name
   - **Last Name**: Your last name
   - **Email Address**: Valid email (important for confirmations)
   - **Password**: Create strong password (mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
   - **Confirm Password**: Retype password to verify
   - **Phone Number**: Your contact number
   - **Country**: Your country of residence
   - **Address**: Your home address
   - **Postal Code**: ZIP/postal code

5. Click **"Create Account"** or **"Sign Up"**

### Email Verification

After creating account:

1. Check your email (including spam folder)
2. Look for verification email from Nephele
3. Click the verification link in email
4. This confirms your email address is correct
5. Your account becomes fully active

### Account Safety Tips

- Use a **strong password** (8+ characters, mix of types)
- Don't share password with others
- Don't use the same password as other websites
- Never give personal information to anyone claiming to be from hotel
- Report suspicious activity immediately

## Logging In

### First-Time Login

1. Go to Nephele login page
2. Enter your **email address** (used to create account)
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click **"Sign In"** button

**Screenshot Reference**: See `sign_in_up.jpg`

**What Happens Next**:
- System logs you in and shows your dashboard
- You may be asked to verify your email if not already done
- Personal information and bookings display

### Returning Guest Login

**If You Have an Existing Account**:

1. Go to login page
2. Use your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. You'll see your bookings and information from previous visits

### Recovering Your Password

**If You Forgot Your Password**:

1. On login page, click **"Forgot Password?"**
2. Enter the email address used for account
3. Check email for reset link (check spam folder too)
4. Click link in email
5. Enter new password
6. Confirm new password
7. Click **"Reset Password"**
8. Log in with new password

### Changing Your Password Anytime

After logging in:

1. Click your name in top-right corner
2. Select **"My Account"** or **"Settings"**
3. Click **"Change Password"**
4. Enter current password
5. Enter new password
6. Confirm new password
7. Click **"Update"**

## Your Bookings & Reservations

### Viewing Your Booking

**See Your Reservation Details**:

1. Click **"My Bookings"** or **"Reservations"** menu
2. See your current and upcoming bookings with:
   - Reservation confirmation number
   - Guest name
   - Check-in date and time
   - Check-out date and time
   - Room type reserved
   - Number of nights
   - Total cost
   - Special requests

3. Click on a booking to see full details

### Booking Confirmation Number

**Why You Need This**:
- Identification at check-in
- Reference for any changes/questions
- For company reimbursement
- To modify booking online

Save your confirmation number:
- Screenshot it
- Write it down
- Save email with confirmation details

### Special Requests

**View Your Special Requests**:

1. Open your booking
2. Scroll to **"Special Requests"** section
3. See any requests you made (early check-in, pet, etc.)
4. These were communicated to the hotel

**Add New Special Request** (before arrival):

1. Open your booking
2. Click **"Add Special Request"** or **"Edit Special Requests"**
3. Type what you need:
   - "Late checkout please"
   - "High floor preferred"
   - "Quiet room needed"
   - "We have a pet with us"
   - "Honeymoon suite requested"
   - "Dietary restrictions: vegetarian"

4. Click **"Submit Request"**
5. Hotel staff will see and try to accommodate

### Modifying Your Booking

**Change Your Dates** (if available):

1. Open your booking
2. Look for **"Modify Booking"** or **"Edit"** button
3. Change check-out date (for extending stay):
   - Select new check-out date
   - See additional cost calculation
   - Process payment
   - Click **"Confirm"**

4. To shorten stay:
   - Select earlier check-out date
   - See refund if applicable
   - Confirm change

**Request Room Change**:

1. Open your booking
2. Click **"Help"** or **"Change Room"**
3. Explain why you want different room
4. Staff will try to accommodate if possible
5. You'll receive confirmation

**Cancel Your Booking:**

1. Open your booking
2. Click **"Cancel Booking"**
3. See refund amount based on cancellation policy
4. Dates to remember:
   - Free cancellation if cancelled by [date]
   - Partial refund if cancelled by [later date]
   - No refund if cancelled close to check-in
5. Confirm cancellation
6. Refund processed if due

### Checking In Online

**Pre-Check-In** (if hotel offers):

1. A few days before arrival, click **"Pre-Check-In"**
2. Complete quick information:
   - Confirm guest count
   - Confirm arrival time
   - Confirm any special requests
3. This speeds up check-in at front desk
4. Front desk staff will have your info ready

## Room Service & Requests

### Requesting Services

**General Service Request Process**:

1. Go to **"Services"** menu option
2. Browse available services:
   - Housekeeping (cleaning, towels, linens)
   - Room service (in-room dining)
   - Concierge (information, directions, recommendations)
   - Maintenance (repair issues)
   - Laundry (clothing service)
   - Spa (massage, wellness services)

3. Click the service you need
4. Fill in details:
   - What specifically you need
   - Any special instructions
   - How urgently needed
   - Best time to come to your room

5. Click **"Submit Request"**
6. See confirmation of request submitted
7. Staff gets notified and responds

### Tracking Service Requests

**Monitor Request Status**:

1. Go to **"My Requests"** or **"Service Requests"**
2. See list of all requests you've submitted
3. Status shows:
   - **Pending**: Not yet responded to
   - **Accepted**: Staff will come
   - **In Progress**: Currently being handled
   - **Completed**: Finished and can rate it

4. Click request to see details and updates

### Rating Services

**After Service Completed**:

1. System may ask you to rate the service
2. Provide feedback:
   - Staff name/description
   - Quality of service (poor/average/good/excellent)
   - Comments about what went well/could improve
3. Your feedback helps hotel improve service

### Guest Requests with Additional Charges

Some services charge extra (not included in room rate):
- Room service food
- Spa treatments
- Premium laundry service
- Extra beds
- Some incidentals

These will be **added to your final bill**. Hotel service asks about charges before delivering.

## Billing & Payments

### Viewing Your Bill

**See Your Charges**:

1. Click **"My Bill"** or **"Invoice"** menu
2. See itemized list of all charges:
   - Room rate per night
   - Number of nights
   - Subtotal for room
   - Any additional charges (room service, minibar, laundry, etc.)
   - Taxes and fees
   - **Total Due**

3. Charges update in real-time as you add services

### Understanding Charges

**Common Charges**:

| Item | What It Is |
|------|-----------|
| **Room Rate** | Nightly rate for your room |
| **Room Service** | Food/beverages ordered to room |
| **Laundry** | Clothing cleaning service |
| **Spa/Wellness** | Massage, treatments, fitness |
| **Activities** | Special events, tours, programs |
| **Parking** | Vehicle parking fees (if applicable) |
| **Service Charges** | Tips/gratuities (if auto-added) |
| **Taxes** | Government taxes on charges |

### Disputing Charges

**Think a Charge is Wrong?**

1. Click the charge you dispute
2. Click **"Dispute This Charge"** or **"Report Error"**
3. Explain why you think it's wrong:
   - I didn't order this
   - Price was different
   - Service wasn't provided
   - Wrong amount charged
4. Submit dispute
5. Hotel manager will review and contact you
6. They'll either confirm charge or remove it

### Payment Methods

**Accepted Payment Methods**:

When you check in or need to pay your bill, the hotel accepts:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AmEx, Discover)
- Debit cards
- Cash (at the time of checkout)
- Bank transfers (if pre-arranged)

**Paying Your Bill Early**:

1. Go to **"My Bill"**
2. See **"Pay Now"** button
3. Select payment method
4. Confirm amount
5. Process payment
6. You get receipt/confirmation

**Note**: You can also pay at checkout when leaving the hotel.

### Invoice & Receipt

**Get Your Invoice**:

1. Before checkout, view **"My Bill"** in Nephele
2. Click **"Download Invoice"** or **"Print Invoice"**
3. System generates PDF of your full charges
4. Save for:
   - Your records
   - Company reimbursement
   - Tax documentation

**After Checkout**:

1. Hotel may email invoice automatically
2. Or you can request it from front desk
3. Invoice shows all stay charges
4. Has hotel details for tax purposes

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Q: I forgot my confirmation number. How do I find it?
**A**: 
1. Log into Nephele → **"My Bookings"**
2. Your confirmation number displays
3. Or check email with original booking confirmation
4. Or call hotel front desk with your name and dates

### Q: Can I check in early?
**A**:
1. Request through Nephele in **"Services"** with "early check-in request"
2. Or call front desk before arrival
3. Availability depends on previous guest checkout
4. Hotel will confirm if possible (may charge extra for partial day)

### Q: Can I extend my stay?
**A**:
1. Log into Nephele → **"My Bookings"**
2. Click **"Extend Stay"** or **"Modify Booking"**
3. Select new check-out date
4. See additional cost
5. Process payment
6. Confirmed immediately if room available

### Q: How do I request a late checkout?
**A**:
1. Go to **"Services"** → **"Special Request"**
2. Type: "Late checkout requested"
3. Specify how late (e.g., "until 2pm")
4. Submit request
5. Hotel will confirm (may charge extra for very late checkout)
6. Or ask front desk in person

### Q: I have a pet with me but didn't mention when booking. Can I keep it?
**A**:
1. Go to **"Services"** → **"Special Request"**
2. Type: "I have a pet - [type and size]"
3. Explain situation
4. Hotel will respond about policy
5. May charge pet fee
6. Or may ask you to make other arrangements

### Q: There's a problem with a service the hotel provided. How do I complain?
**A**:
1. Go to **"Services"** → **"Report Issue"** or **"Feedback"**
2. Describe the problem
3. Be specific (staff name, time, what happened)
4. Explain how it affected you
5. Suggest solution if applicable
6. Submit complaint
7. Manager will follow up with you

### Q: The Wi-Fi isn't working in my room. What should I do?
**A**:
1. Try restarting your device
2. Reconnect to Wi-Fi network
3. Go to **"Services"** → **"Report Issue"** → **"Wi-Fi Problem"**
4. Describe the issue
5. IT staff will investigate/fix
6. Or go to front desk for immediate help

### Q: I want to book a restaurant in the hotel. How do I do that?
**A**:
1. Click **"Services"** → **"Restaurant"** or **"Dining"**
2. Browse restaurants or restaurants options
3. Check available times
4. Click **"Make Reservation"**
5. Select time and party size
6. Confirm details
7. Get confirmation number

### Q: What happens if I damage something in the room?
**A**:
1. Report it to front desk immediately
2. Or go to **"Services"** → **"Report Damage"**
3. Explain what happened
4. If accidental, hotel may not charge (depends on damage)
5. If deliberate or negligent, you may be charged for repairs
6. Discuss with manager to clarify

### Q: Are there additional fees I should know about?
**A**:
1. Your room rate includes: Bed, basic amenities
2. Additional fees may apply to:
   - Parking
   - Room service food
   - Spa services
   - Laundry
   - Activities
   - Extra beds
   - Pet fees (if applicable)
3. All charges show on your bill before checkout
4. Ask front desk if unsure

### Q: Can I request a specific room?
**A**:
1. You can request a preference, but not guarantee
2. Go to **"Services"** → **"Special Request"**
3. Request: "High floor preferred" or "Quiet room please"
4. Hotel will try to accommodate but cannot promise
5. Based on availability at check-in

### Q: How do I request a room with a better view?
**A**:
1. After check-in, go to front desk
2. Ask nicely if better view room is available
3. They may move you (no guarantee)
4. Or request before arrival in **"Special Request"**
5. No extra charge unless it's a higher room category

### Q: The bill seems too high. How do I review charges?
**A**:
1. Go to **"My Bill"** in Nephele
2. Review each charge
3. Calculate: (room rate × nights) + services + taxes
4. If something looks wrong, click to **"Dispute Charge"**
5. Or ask front desk to review with you

### Q: Can I change my departure date?
**A**:
1. Log into Nephele → **"My Bookings"**
2. Click **"Modify Booking"**
3. Change checkout date
4. See new total cost (extension) or refund (early departure)
5. Process payment/refund
6. Confirmation shows new dates

### Q: What if I need to leave earlier than planned?
**A**:
1. Go to **"My Bookings"**
2. Click **"Modify Booking"** → **"Change Check-out Date"**
3. Select new (earlier) check-out date
4. See refund amount (depends on cancellation policy)
5. Confirm early departure
6. Check out and process final bill

### Q: How do I know hotel received my service request?
**A**:
1. After submitting request, look for confirmation message
2. Go to **"My Requests"** to see all requests
3. Status shows "Received" → "In Progress" → "Completed"
4. You may also get notification when staff responds

### Q: Is my information secure? Is it safe to use Nephele?
**A**:
1. Hotel takes security seriously
2. Your personal data is encrypted
3. Use strong password (8+ characters, mixed types)
4. Don't share password with others
5. Log out when using public computers
6. Never give credit card info except in secure checkout
7. Report any suspicious activity immediately
