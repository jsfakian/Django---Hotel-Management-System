### 3. Customer Needs Assessment

**Objective:** Validate and prioritize customer needs

**Research Methods:**
- [ ] Structured interviews (10-15 hotel managers)
- [ ] Structured interviews (8-12 travel agencies)
- [ ] Online surveys (50-100 respondents)
- [ ] Focus groups (2-3 sessions)
- [ ] Receptionist feedback sessions

**Questions to Address:**
- What are the biggest operational challenges?
- What features are most important?
- What is the current booking process?
- How is pricing currently managed?
- What payment systems are used?
- How is staff managed?
- Reporting and analytics needs?
- Mobile access requirements?
- Integration needs?
- Budget and ROI expectations?

**Pain Points to Validate:**
- [ ] Manual booking processes
- [ ] Lack of dynamic pricing
- [ ] Poor availability management
- [ ] Invoice and payment delays
- [ ] Difficulty managing contracts
- [ ] Time-consuming reporting
- [ ] Limited guest communication
- [ ] Staff scheduling complexity

**Deliverable:** Customer Needs Assessment

### 6. Pricing Strategy Research

**Objective:** Develop optimal pricing model

**Analysis Topics:**

1. **Willingness to Pay**
   - [ ] Customer surveys
   - [ ] Price sensitivity analysis
   - [ ] Budget availability research

2. **Pricing Models to Evaluate**
   - [ ] SaaS subscription (per user, per property, per booking)
   - [ ] Tiered pricing (basic, professional, enterprise)
   - [ ] Usage-based pricing (per transaction)
   - [ ] Hybrid models
   - [ ] One-time license + support

3. **Competitor Pricing Comparison**
   - [ ] Entry-level pricing
   - [ ] Mid-tier pricing
   - [ ] Premium pricing
   - [ ] Discounts and bundles

4. **Value-Based Pricing**
   - [ ] Cost savings generated for customers
   - [ ] Revenue increase potential
   - [ ] Overall ROI to customer
   - [ ] Payback period expectations

**Recommended Pricing Model (from Business Plan):**

| Service | Annual Subscription |
|---------|-------------------|
| Booking Management | €600 |
| Contract Management | €600 |
| Payment Processing | €600 |
| Occupancy Management | €1,000 |
| Admin Reporting | €500 |
| AI Dynamic Pricing | €1,000 |

- Apply discounts for early adopters (15-20%)
- Offer bundle pricing options
- Annual subscription discount (pay annually, save 10%)
