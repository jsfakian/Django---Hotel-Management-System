# Task 1: Feasibility Study (WP.01)

**Phase:** Phase 1 - Planning & Research  
**Duration:** 3 months (Month 1-3)  
**Team Lead:** External Consultant  
**Status:** ⏳ Not Started

---

## Objective

Conduct a comprehensive feasibility study to analyze the viability of the NEPHELE hotel management system. This study will evaluate business problems, identify solutions, assess economic feasibility, and determine market opportunities.

---

## Scope

### In Scope
- Business problem analysis
- Current market solutions review
- Alternative approach evaluation
- Economic feasibility assessment
- Technical feasibility review
- Risk identification
- Resource requirement analysis
- Implementation timeline estimation

### Out of Scope
- Detailed system design (handled in Phase 2)
- Market research depth (separate task)
- Prototype development

---

## Key Activities

### 1. Business Problem Analysis

**Objective:** Identify and document business problems the NEPHELE system will solve

- [ ] Interview hotel management and travel agencies
- [ ] Document current pain points:
  - Manual booking processes
  - Lack of dynamic pricing capabilities
  - Inefficient contract management
  - Time-consuming administrative tasks
- [ ] Analyze impact of problems on business operations
- [ ] Quantify efficiency losses

**Deliverable:** Business Problem Report

### 2. Current Solutions Review

**Objective:** Evaluate existing hotel management systems

- [ ] Research competing products (property management systems)
- [ ] Identify gaps in current solutions
- [ ] Compare features and capabilities
- [ ] Review pricing models
- [ ] Analyze market coverage (SMB vs Enterprise)
- [ ] Document lessons learned from competitors

**Deliverable:** Competitive Analysis Document

### 3. Alternative Approaches Evaluation

**Objective:** Assess different implementation strategies

**Options to evaluate:**

Option 1: Monolithic Architecture
- Pros: Simpler initial development, unified codebase
- Cons: Scaling challenges long-term

Option 2: Microservices Architecture
- Pros: Better scalability, component independence
- Cons: Increased complexity, deployment challenges

Option 3: Hybrid Approach
- Pros: Balance between simplicity and scalability
- Cons: Medium complexity

Option 4: White-label Solution (Outsourcing)
- Pros: Faster time-to-market
- Cons: Less control, higher costs, limited customization

**For Each Option:**
- [ ] Technical feasibility assessment
- [ ] Cost estimation
- [ ] Implementation timeline
- [ ] Long-term maintainability
- [ ] Scalability potential

**Deliverable:** Alternative Solutions Analysis

### 4. Economic Feasibility

**Objective:** Determine financial viability

Analyze:
- [ ] Development costs (labor, infrastructure, tools)
- [ ] Operational costs (hosting, support, maintenance)
- [ ] Revenue projections (conservative, moderate, optimistic scenarios)
- [ ] Break-even analysis
- [ ] ROI calculations
- [ ] Pricing strategy feasibility
- [ ] Customer acquisition costs
- [ ] Market size and addressable revenue

**Financial Scenarios to Model:**

| Scenario | Customers Y1 | Revenue | Break-even | Notes |
|----------|-------------|---------|-----------|-------|
| Conservative | 200-300 | €400k-€500k | Month 24 | Safe estimate |
| Moderate | 400-500 | €800k-€1M | Month 18 | Realistic |
| Optimistic | 600-800 | €1.2M-€1.5M | Month 12 | Aggressive |

**Deliverable:** Financial Feasibility Report

### 5. Technical Feasibility

**Objective:** Assess technical implementation viability

- [ ] Technology stack evaluation
- [ ] Team skill gap analysis
- [ ] Development tool assessment
- [ ] Infrastructure requirements
- [ ] Data security and compliance feasibility (GDPR)
- [ ] Integration complexity with third-party systems
- [ ] Performance and scalability technical assessment

**Areas:**
- [ ] Django framework suitability
- [ ] PostgreSQL reliability
- [ ] Cloud infrastructure options
- [ ] AI/ML algorithm feasibility
- [ ] API integration capability

**Deliverable:** Technical Feasibility Report

### 6. Market Opportunity Analysis

**Objective:** Validate market demand and opportunity

- [ ] Greek hotel market size (4,000+ active hotels)
- [ ] European market potential
- [ ] North American market size
- [ ] Growth trends in hotel technology adoption
- [ ] Travel agency segment opportunities
- [ ] Digital transformation momentum in hospitality

**Market Data to Gather:**
- [ ] Number of target hotels in Greece
- [ ] Average hotel staff size
- [ ] Current technology spending
- [ ] AI/ML adoption rates
- [ ] SaaS adoption trends

**Deliverable:** Market Opportunity Assessment

### 7. Risk Identification & Analysis

**Objective:** Identify major risks to project success

**Risk Categories:**

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|-----------|
| Technology adoption resistance | Medium | High | Training, demos, gradual rollout |
| Market saturation | Low | Medium | Differentiation strategy |
| Data security incidents | Low | Critical | Strong security measures |
| Scalability issues | Low | Medium | Cloud architecture |
| Developer retention | Medium | Medium | Competitive compensation |
| Regulatory changes | Low | Medium | Legal compliance review |

**Deliverable:** Risk Register

### 8. Resource Assessment

**Objective:** Determine resource requirements

- [ ] Team composition and skills needed
- [ ] Hardware and software requirements
- [ ] Training and capacity building needs
- [ ] Budget allocation
- [ ] Timeline feasibility with available resources
