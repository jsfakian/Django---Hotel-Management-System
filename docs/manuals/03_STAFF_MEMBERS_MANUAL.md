## Getting Started

### What is Nephele?

Nephele is a hotel management system that helps our hotel run smoothly by:
- Tracking room status and maintenance needs
- Organizing staff schedules and tasks
- Managing guest information and special requests
- Processing bookings and payments
- Communicating announcements and messages

As a staff member, the system helps you:
- Know what tasks you need to complete
- Track your work schedule
- Submit time off requests
- See important announcements
- Communicate with your team

### System Requirements

- **Device**: Computer, tablet, or smartphone
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
- **Internet**: Access to your hotel network or internet
- **Account**: Provided by property manager or administrator

## Logging In

### Initial Login

When your account is created, you'll receive an email with:
- Your username
- A temporary password
- Login URL or instructions

**First-Time Login Process**:

1. Open the provided login URL or go to Nephele system
2. Enter your **username**
3. Enter your **temporary password**
4. Click **"Sign In"**

You'll be prompted to:
- Verify your email address
- Change your temporary password
- Complete your profile (phone number, address, etc.)

**Screenshot Reference**: See `sign_in_up.jpg` in the Screenshots folder

### Changing Your Password

After initial login, you should set a personal password:

1. Click your name in the top-right corner
2. Select **"My Profile"** or **"Settings"**
3. Click **"Change Password"**
4. Enter current password
5. Enter new password (use a strong password with letters, numbers, special characters)
6. Confirm new password
7. Click **"Update Password"**

### Forgotten Password

If you forget your password:

1. On the login page, click **"Forgot Password?"**
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a reset link
4. Click the link in the email
5. Enter your new password
6. Log back in with new password

### Accessing the System from Home

If your property provides remote access:
1. Use the same URL and credentials
2. Login from any device with internet
3. Access will be restricted based on your role and location
4. Some features may be limited when off-property

## Understanding Your Schedule

### Viewing Your Schedule

**Check Your Work Schedule**:

1. Click **"My Schedule"** or **"Schedule"** from menu
2. Choose view option:
   - **Week View**: See your full work week (7 days)
   - **Month View**: See full month schedule
   - **List View**: See shifts listed as a table

3. Your schedule shows:
   - **Shift Dates**: Dates you're scheduled
   - **Shift Times**: Start and end times
   - **Shift Type**: Day shift, evening shift, night shift
   - **Location**: Where you work (front desk, housekeeping, kitchen, etc.)

### Understanding Shift Information

**Shift Details**:

| Information | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **Start Time** | When your shift begins (when you should arrive) |
| **End Time** | When your shift ends (when you can leave) |
| **Duration** | Total hours for that shift |
| **Break Time** | Scheduled breaks during shift |
| **Department** | Which team/location you work |

### Schedule Color Coding

Different colors may indicate:
- **Green**: Your regular scheduled shift
- **Blue**: Special shift or temporary assignment
- **Yellow**: On-call availability
- **Red**: Called off (shift cancelled, no need to come in)
- **Gray**: Days off/not scheduled

### Shift Changes & Swaps

**Request Schedule Change** (if system allows):

1. Click on the shift you want to change
2. Select **"Request Change"** or **"Request Swap"**
3. Enter reason for request
4. Select employee you want to trade shifts with (if swap) or leave blank for manager assignment
5. Click **"Submit Request"**
6. Wait for approval from your manager

**Note**: Not all properties allow employee-initiated swaps. Check with your manager.

## Task Management

### Viewing Assigned Tasks

**See Tasks Assigned to You**:

1. Click **"My Tasks"** or **"Tasks"** from menu
2. See list of tasks with:
   - Task type (Housekeeping, Maintenance, Service, etc.)
   - Description of task
   - Priority level
   - Due date/time
   - Status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed)

### Task Priorities

**Understanding Task Priority**:

| Priority | Color | Meaning |
|----------|-------|---------|
| **Low** | Blue | Can be done anytime, no rush |
| **Medium** | Green | Should be done during normal duties |
| **High** | Orange | Important, should be done soon |
| **Urgent** | Red | Critical, must be done immediately |

### Completing Tasks

**Mark Task as Started**:

1. Click on the task in the list
2. See full task details
3. Click **"Start Task"** or **"Mark In Progress"**
4. System records when task started

**Update Task Progress** (if applicable):

1. While working on task, you may be able to:
   - Add notes about progress
   - Attach photos (especially for maintenance work)
   - Note any issues encountered

2. Click **"Add Update"** or **"Add Note"** to log progress

**Complete Task**:

1. When task is finished:
   - Click **"Mark Complete"** or **"Complete Task"**
   - If requested, add completion notes
   - Rate quality (optional feedback): Good, Average, Needs Work
   - Click **"Submit"**

2. Task status changes to "Completed"
3. Manager gets notification task is done

### Task Examples by Role

**Housekeeping Tasks**:
- Clean rooms (specific room numbers listed)
- Change linens
- Restock supplies
- Vacuum carpets
- Handle laundry

**Maintenance Tasks**:
- Fix broken items
- Perform preventive maintenance
- Respond to maintenance requests
- Safety inspections

**Food Service Tasks**:
- Food preparation
- Inventory management
- Equipment cleaning
- Meal service setup

**Reception Tasks**:
- Guest check-in assistance
- Phone coverage
- Administrative work

### Escalating Problems

**If You Can't Complete a Task**:

1. Click on the task
2. Click **"Report Issue"** or **"Request Help"**
3. Explain the problem:
   - Missing equipment
   - Damage to area
   - Medical/safety issue
   - Unable to access location
4. Click **"Submit"**
5. Manager will be notified and may reassign or help

## Timekeeping & Attendance

### Clocking In & Out

**If your property uses Timekeeping**:

1. At start of shift, click **"Clock In"**
2. Confirm you're present and ready for work
3. At end of shift, click **"Clock Out"**
4. System records hours worked

**Note**: Some properties use physical time clocks instead. Ask your manager which method your property uses.

### Viewing Your Time Records

**Check Hours Worked**:

1. Click **"My Time"** or **"Hours"**
2. See record of:
   - Dates worked
   - Start times and end times
   - Total hours each day
   - Weekly hours total

3. Verify accuracy:
   - Are all shifts recorded?
   - Are times correct?
   - Any shifts missing?

**Report Time Discrepancies**:

If you notice an error:
1. Click on the date with error
2. Click **"Report Time Error"** or **"Dispute Time"**
3. Explain what time is wrong
4. Suggest correct time (with reason if needed)
5. Submit request
6. Manager will review and correct if valid

### Attendance Tracking

**Attendance includes**:
- Days you worked as scheduled (present)
- Days you requested off and were approved (excused absence)
- Days you didn't show up without notice (unexcused absence)

**Maintain Good Attendance**:
- Arrive on time for scheduled shifts
- Provide notice if you must call in sick
- Notify manager of schedule conflicts
- Request time off in advance

## Submitting Leave Requests

### Types of Leave

**Leave Types** (available depends on your employment):

| Type | Use For |
|------|---------|
| **Vacation/PTO** | Planned time off for personal use |
| **Sick Leave** | When you're ill and can't work |
| **Personal Time** | Personal appointments/emergencies |
| **Family Leave** | Family emergencies or needs |
| **Bereavement** | Death of family member |
| **Unpaid Leave** | Time off without pay (if approved) |

### Requesting Leave

**Submit a Leave Request**:

1. Go to **"Leave Management"** or **"Submit Leave Request"**
2. Click **"New Leave Request"**
3. Fill in details:
   - **Leave Type**: Select type from dropdown
   - **Start Date**: First day of leave
   - **End Date**: Last day of leave
   - **Reason** (optional): Brief explanation
   - **Contact During Leave**: Emergency contact number

4. Click **"Submit Request"**
5. Your manager will receive notification
6. You'll get email when approved or denied

### Checking Leave Balance

**View Available Time Off**:

1. Click **"Leave Balance"** or **"My Time Off"**
2. See:
   - Total vacation/PTO days available
   - Total days used so far this year
   - Days remaining
   - Days that will be available soon

3. Some properties reset balances yearly, monthly, or have rolling balances

### Emergency Leave

**If you need immediate time off**:

1. Call your manager as soon as possible
2. Then submit leave request through system
3. Mark as **"Emergency Leave"** if available
4. Provide explanation
5. Manager may approve or suggest alternatives (unpaid leave)

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Q: I can't log into the system.
**A**:
1. Check if you have correct URL
2. Verify spelling of username
3. Ensure "Caps Lock" is not on when typing password
4. If still can't log in, contact your manager or administrator

### Q: I don't see any tasks assigned to me.
**A**:
1. Your manager may not have assigned tasks yet for this period
2. Check if you have an upcoming shift (tasks may appear day before)
3. Contact your manager to see if you have tasks

### Q: I worked extra hours but they're not showing in my time record.
**A**:
1. Check if you clocked in/out correctly
2. Verify times in system match your memory
3. If times are wrong, submit "Report Time Error"
4. Contact your manager or payroll for discrepancies

### Q: I requested leave but got no response.
**A**:
1. Check if request has status (Pending, Approved, Denied)
2. If pending, check back in a few days
3. Contact your manager if overdue (should respond within 3 days)
4. If urgent, ask manager in person

### Q: I can't complete a task because the equipment is broken.
**A**:
1. Click on task and select **"Report Issue"**
2 Explain that equipment is broken
3. Submit request
4. Manager will either:
   - Fix the issue
   - Reassign the task
   - Provide alternative equipment

### Q: There's a safety issue in a room (broken glass, spill, etc.).
**A**:
1. **Do not attempt to fix if dangerous**
2. Mark task as **"Report Issue"**
3. Clearly explain the safety hazard
4. Manager will prioritize immediately
5. You may be reassigned to different area
6. Don't work in unsafe conditions

### Q: I haven't received my leave request approval.
**A**:
1. Check the request status
2. If "Pending", wait a bit more
3. If no status, resubmit request
4. Contact manager directly if urgent
5. May take several days for approval

### Q: How do I know when I'm scheduled to work?
**A**:  
1. Check **"My Schedule"** at least weekly
2. You can set calendar reminders/alarms
3. Ask your manager to send schedule ahead of time
4. Check announcements for schedule changes

### Q: Can I swap shifts with another staff member?
**A**:
1. Depends on your property's policy
2. If allowed, click on shift and select **"Request Swap"**
3. Select colleague you want to trade with
4. Both staff members and manager must approve
5. Schedule will update once approved

### Q: Who do I contact for technical support with the system?
**A**:
1. Ask your manager first
2. They can submit IT support ticket
3. Or contact system administrator
4. Avoid trying to "fix" system yourself

### Q: I forgot when I'm scheduled for my next shift.
**A**:
1. Click **"My Schedule"**
2. Check the calendar or list view
3. See all upcoming scheduled shifts
4. Mark them down or set phone reminders

### Q: The system says I'm "unexcused absent" but I called in sick.
**A**:
1. Check if manager updated system
2. Request manager mark as "excused absence"
3. Provide documentation if required (doctor's note)
4. Don't leave it unresolved as it affects your record

### Q: Can I see other staff members' schedules?
**A**:
Usually no - your system access is limited to your own information. However:
1. Some properties show team schedules (ask manager)
2. Team schedules may be posted at workplace
3. You can ask manager who's working when

## Best Practices

1. **Check Schedule Weekly**: Know when you're working
2. **Log In On Time**: Arrive for shifts with time to settle in
3. **Complete Tasks Thoroughly**: Do quality work first time
4. **Report Issues Early**: Tell manager about problems right away
5. **Maintain Safety**: Follow all safety procedures and protocols
6. **Teamwork**: Help colleagues when they need help
7. **Professional Appearance**: Meet uniform and appearance standards
8. **Communicate**: Tell manager about problems, don't just complain
9. **Use System**: Keep time records accurate for payroll accuracy
10. **Ask Questions**: It's better to ask than guess on procedures
