| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|----------|-----------|

| **Long response times** | High | SLA monitoring, resource planning, prioritization |

| **Knowledge gaps** | Medium | Documentation, training, expertise development |

- Tiered SLA degradation process
- Contractor/temporary staff sourcing
- Workflow automation acceleration
- Customer communication protocol

- Cross-training for multi-tier support
- On-call temporary staffing
- Overtime procedures
- Customer self-service emphasis
